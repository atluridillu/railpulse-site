Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 July 2026  ·  Last updated: 28 July 2026  ·  Developer: Dillu Atluri

This Privacy Policy explains what information RailPulse ("the App", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

Disclaimer: RailPulse is an independent application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Indian Railways, IRCTC, or the Ministry of Railways, Government of India. Train and PNR information is sourced from third-party data providers and is provided for convenience only. Always verify critical journey details through official railway channels.

1. Information We Collect

a) Location data (including background location)

Prominent disclosure: The App collects location data to power the station alarm feature — to wake you before your train reaches your station — even when the App is closed or not in use. Background location is collected only while a station alarm is on, and stops when the alarm fires, is dismissed, or is turned off.
  • Your device's GPS position is processed on your device to calculate the distance to your station and to show your position on the map.
  • Location data is not stored on our servers, not sold, and not shared with third parties. It is used in real time on your device and discarded.
  • You can revoke location permission at any time in your device settings. The destination alarm and map features will stop working, but the rest of the App remains usable.

b) PNR and journey information

  • When you enter a PNR number or train number, it is sent to our railway data provider, IRCTC1 (via RapidAPI), to retrieve booking status, train schedules, and live running status.
  • PNR numbers and saved trips are stored locally on your device so you can re-open them quickly. We do not store your PNR or booking details on our own servers.

c) Emergency contact

  • If you save a personal emergency contact for the SOS feature, the name and phone number are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded, shared, or transmitted to us or any third party. Tapping the SOS ball opens your phone's dialer to call a helpline or your saved contact; long-pressing it opens your messaging app with a pre-filled text to your saved contact that includes your live GPS location — the App does not send this message itself, and you must tap Send yourself.

d) Device and diagnostic information

  • The App does not use any analytics or crash-reporting service. We do not collect app usage statistics, device identifiers, or crash reports.

e) What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, Aadhaar, payment information, contacts list, photos, or messages.
  • We do not require an account or sign-up to use the App.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • IRCTC1 (via RapidAPI) — railway data (train status, PNR status, schedules). RapidAPI Privacy Policy
  • CARTO — map tile display (the App does not use Google Maps). CARTO Privacy Policy
  • OpenStreetMap — underlying map data for the tiles above. OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy

Loading a map tile or fetching train data sends your IP address and the relevant request (e.g. tile coordinates, PNR/train number) to that provider's servers, governed by their own privacy policy linked above. We do not control what those providers do with that request beyond fulfilling it.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information with anyone. We do not share data with advertisers.

4. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

  • Saved trips, PNRs, settings, and your emergency contact live only in the App's local storage on your device.
  • You can delete all locally stored data at any time via Settings → Clear App Data inside the App, or by clearing the App's storage / uninstalling the App from your device.
  • Because we do not operate accounts or servers storing your personal data, uninstalling the App removes all data we hold about you on that device.

5. Security

Communication with our data provider occurs over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Locally stored data is protected by your device's standard app sandboxing. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the data handled.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Permissions: You may grant or revoke location, notification, and other permissions at any time in your device settings.
  • Deletion: Clear the App's data or uninstall it to erase all locally held information.
  • Grievances (India): In accordance with applicable Indian law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may contact our grievance officer with any concern: Dillu Atluri, atluridillu@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will assist in removing it.

8. Emergency Services Notice

The SOS feature places calls through your phone's dialer to helpline numbers (such as railway and medical helplines) or your saved contact, and can pre-fill a text message with your live location to your saved contact through your phone's messaging app. We are not responsible for the availability or response of any helpline, emergency service, or your contact. In a life-threatening emergency, always contact official emergency services directly.
